## 🌟 About Me

Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I am **Dr. T. V. Sai Krishna**, a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at **ACE Engineering College, Hyderabad, Telangana, India**.

With over **21 years of teaching and research experience**, I am passionate about advancing knowledge in emerging computing technologies, conducting impactful research, mentoring students, and contributing to academic and technological innovation.

My academic and research journey focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Quantum Computing, and Cloud Computing.

- 🎓 **Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering** – JNTUK (2018)
- 🏛️ **Current Position:** Professor, CSE, ACE Engineering College
- 👨‍🏫 **Experience:** 21+ Years in Teaching and Research
- 📚 **Research Publications:** Journal and conference publications
- 📖 **Academic Author:** Books on Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence
- 💡 **Patents:** 2 Patents
- 🔬 **Research Focus:** Intelligent Computing and Emerging Technologies
